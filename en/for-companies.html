Healthcare expenditures, investments, and financing are among the top priorities for every country and remain an ongoing focus of development. With an aging population, advancements in medical technology, and increasing expectations from healthcare services, healthcare spending and investments continue to grow steadily each year.

However, healthcare investments and management differ significantly from other industries:

  • They are high-cost investments.
  • They are difficult to convert to other sectors.
  • They have a longer return on investment (ROI) period.
  • They are subject to strict regulations and compliance requirements.

Healthcare financing is increasingly moving toward a public–private balance model, with out-of-pocket spending and private health insurance playing a growing role in this process.

Therefore, when investing in the healthcare sector, it is essential to understand the country’s market dynamics, reimbursement systems, and core healthcare regulations.
