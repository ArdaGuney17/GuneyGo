Good to Know

  • Implant warrantyImplants are covered by a lifetime manufacturer's warranty. Only the procedure fee is charged for any necessary implant replacements.
  • Duration of stayThe recommended length of stay following the implant procedure is one week. A second visit may be required for the implant process.
  • Healing periodThe healing period lasts between 2 and 3 months, depending on the treatment site.
  • Duration of stay for the second visitA stay of 1–2 weeks is required for the second visit, depending on the number of prostheses to be fitted.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the price list include?

The prices above are per-procedure fees from one of our partner dental clinics in Istanbul, valid through 31 December 2026. Your final treatment plan and total cost are confirmed after a review of your case.

Are dental implants guaranteed?

Yes — implants carry a lifetime manufacturer's warranty. If a replacement is ever needed, you only pay the procedure fee, not the implant itself.

How long do I need to stay in Turkey?

For most implant treatments, a one-week stay is recommended, with a possible second visit 2–3 months later once healing is complete, for the final prosthesis.

Can I get a full treatment plan before I travel?

Yes. Share your dental records or photos with us for a free, personalized treatment plan and quote before booking your trip.
