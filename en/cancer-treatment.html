How Pricing Works

Cancer treatment cannot be given a fixed, one-size-fits-all price — it depends on diagnosis, stage and the treatment plan your oncologist recommends. After our team reviews your medical file, you receive a personalized treatment plan and cost estimate before deciding whether to travel.

We do not publish cure rates, success guarantees, or promises of a specific outcome on this page — treatment results depend on your individual diagnosis. Our role is to help you access the right specialists, coordinate your care, and support a second opinion.
